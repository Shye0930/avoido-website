Avoido uses LiDAR depth to show nearby measured surfaces and provide close-range cues.

Meet the prototype →

  1. Scan

    LiDAR measures surfaces in the device's view.

  2. Map

    Visual maps distinguish measured surfaces from uncertain space.

  3. Respond

    Close-range speech and supported haptics provide proximity cues.

Red: supported obstacles Green: sampled visibility or confirmed ground Yellow: unknown regions

Green does not establish a safe route. Camera distance-point colors have a different meaning from map-region colors.
